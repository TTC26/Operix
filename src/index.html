Questions

What is a DPR for a bank loan?

A Detailed Project Report explains the project, promoters, market, cost, means of finance and projected financials so a bank can judge whether the loan can be repaid. Bizvora builds it from your inputs and calculates every figure. Read the DPR guide.

Does Bizvora prepare CMA data?

Yes. Mode 3 produces CMA forms with historical and projected operating statements, balance sheet analysis, current assets and liabilities, MPBF (Tandon Method I and II) and fund flow. Read the CMA guide.

How much does it cost?

Building the report and the Excel model is free. The final PDF and Word report costs ₹599 for a General DPR, ₹999 for a Bank/MSME DPR and ₹1,499 with CMA data, per project.

Will the bank approve my loan if I use Bizvora?

No software can promise approval. Bizvora checks your numbers against common lender benchmarks such as DSCR and current ratio, so you can fix weak points before you apply.

Can I use currencies other than rupees?

Yes. You can choose from about 30 currencies and show amounts in lakh/crore or thousand/million.
