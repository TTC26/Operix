Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Oryqen Technologies (sole proprietorship) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address.
  • Project data you enter: company, promoter, financial and project information.
  • Payment records: order and payment IDs and amounts from Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service securely.

How we use it

To provide and secure the service, generate your reports, process payments, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored

Accounts and projects are stored with Google Firebase. Payments are processed by Razorpay. If you use AI drafting, the relevant project details are sent to our AI provider to produce the text.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your projects at any time. To delete your account and data, email info.bizvora@gmail.com.
