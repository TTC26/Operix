Project report for an MSME loan

Micro, small and medium enterprises apply for term loans, working capital and government-backed schemes with a project report. The scheme decides the paperwork, but the core of every report is the same: cost, funding and the ability to repay.

What to include

  • Business details: constitution, registration numbers, Udyam registration, location.
  • Promoter background, experience and own contribution.
  • Products or services, capacity and a realistic ramp-up of capacity utilisation.
  • Cost of project: machinery with quotations, building or rent, pre-operative costs and margin money for working capital.
  • Means of finance: promoter share, term loan, working capital, subsidy if any.
  • Projected profitability, cash flow and balance sheet.
  • DSCR, break-even and a repayment schedule that includes any moratorium.

Scheme notes

Government schemes such as Mudra, CGTMSE credit guarantee and PMEGP have their own eligibility rules, loan limits, subsidy rates and application portals, and these change from time to time. Check the current rules on the official scheme website or with your bank branch before you finalise the project cost and means of finance. The project report itself should still show clear, consistent numbers.

Checklist before you submit

  • Project cost equals means of finance.
  • Promoter contribution is shown and can be proved (bank statements, net-worth statement).
  • Every machine has a quotation, including freight, installation and taxes.
  • Sales never exceed installed capacity.
  • DSCR is above your bank's minimum in every repayment year.
  • Licences and approvals needed for your sector are listed with their status.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum promoter contribution for an MSME loan?

It depends on the bank and the scheme. Many term loans expect a promoter margin in the range of 10% to 25% of project cost; check your lender's norms.

Can I get a loan without collateral?

Some MSME loans are covered by credit guarantee schemes such as CGTMSE. Eligibility and limits are set by the scheme and the lender.

Which report mode should I use in Bizvora?

Use Mode 2 (Bank / MSME DPR) for a new unit, and Mode 3 (with CMA data) if your business already has past financial statements.
