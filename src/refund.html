Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Each payment unlocks the final report for one project and is delivered immediately.

Refunds

  • If you were charged but the report did not unlock, or you were charged twice, email info.bizvora@gmail.com with your payment ID within 7 days. We will unlock the report or refund the duplicate or failed charge.
  • If the unlocked PDF or Word report cannot be generated because of a fault on our side that we cannot fix within 5 working days, we will refund the payment.
  • Because the report is a digital product available as soon as you pay, payments are not refundable once the report has been downloaded, except in the cases above.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days.

Cancellation

There is no subscription to cancel. You pay only when you unlock a report.
