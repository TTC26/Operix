CMA data for bank loans: forms, MPBF and format

Credit Monitoring Arrangement (CMA) data is the set of financial statements banks use to assess working capital and term loans. It puts past and projected figures side by side, so the banker can see the trend and the limit the business can support.

The CMA forms

FormContents
Form IExisting and proposed banking arrangements
Form IIOperating statement: sales, costs, profit, for past and projected years
Form IIIAnalysis of balance sheet: net worth, term liabilities, current assets and liabilities
Form IVComparative statement of current assets and current liabilities, with holding periods
Form VComputation of Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (MPBF)
Form VIFund flow statement

Bank formats differ slightly, but the content is the same. Most banks want the last 2 to 3 audited years, the current year estimate and projections for the loan period.

How MPBF is calculated

Working capital gap = total current assets − current liabilities other than bank borrowing.

  • Tandon Method I: MPBF = 75% of the working capital gap. The promoter funds the other 25% of the gap.
  • Tandon Method II: MPBF = 75% of total current assets − current liabilities other than bank borrowing. The promoter funds 25% of current assets, so the bank finance is lower.
  • Turnover method: used for smaller MSME limits, where the limit is commonly based on 20% of projected turnover with a 5% promoter margin.

Which method applies depends on the bank and the size of the limit.

What bankers check in CMA data

  • Sales growth in projections compared with the past three years.
  • Inventory and receivable days, and whether they are getting longer.
  • Current ratio and TOL/TNW (total outside liabilities to tangible net worth).
  • Whether turnover in the books matches GST returns, ITR and bank credits.

Frequently asked questions

Is CMA data needed for a term loan?

Many banks ask for it for term loans as well as working capital, especially for existing businesses and larger limits.

How many years of data go into CMA?

Typically 2 to 3 past audited years, the current year estimate, and projections for the loan tenure.

Can I prepare CMA data without a CA?

You can prepare the data yourself, but banks usually want audited financials and often a CA-certified CMA for larger limits.
