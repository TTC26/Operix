Pricing

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Bizvora is free to use for building project reports, running the financial model and downloading the Excel model. You pay only when you want the final report for a project.

ReportPrice per project
Mode 1 · General DPR₹599
Mode 2 · Bank / MSME DPR₹999
Mode 3 · Bank / MSME + CMA₹1,499

One payment unlocks the PDF and editable Word report for that project. You can edit and download it again at no extra cost. If you move a paid project to a higher mode, you pay only the difference. Prices are in Indian rupees and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. Payments are processed by Razorpay.
