Privacy Policy

Short version: Your business data is yours. We store it securely, never sell it, never use it for advertising, and you can export or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Operix ("we", "us", "our") is a business management platform that helps companies manage invoices, purchases, inventory, and operations. Our platform is accessible at operix.app (or the domain where you access this service).

2. What data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account data: Your email address and password (hashed — we never see your plain-text password).
  • Business profile: Company name, address, GSTIN/tax ID, logo, and contact details that you enter in Settings.
  • Business records: Documents (invoices, purchase orders, quotations, etc.), customers, vendors, items, stock movements, vouchers, payroll, and any other data you create inside Operix.
  • Usage data: We may log basic usage events (page visits, errors) to improve the product. No behavioural tracking or ad profiling is done.

We do not collect: payment card details, Aadhaar numbers, or any sensitive personal data beyond what you voluntarily enter.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain the Operix service.
  • To sync your data across devices via Firebase (Google Cloud).
  • To send transactional emails (account verification, password reset).
  • To improve product reliability and fix bugs.

We will never use your data to serve you advertisements, build marketing profiles, or sell to third parties.

4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud Firestore, hosted in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region.

  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 encryption for all stored data.
  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.2+ for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Access control: Each user's data is isolated by a unique owner UID. Staff accounts can only access data belonging to their company owner.
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication with email verification. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business data with third parties except:

  • Google Cloud (Firebase): Our infrastructure provider. Google processes data per their Data Processing Addendum and is GDPR-compliant.
  • Legal obligation: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government authority (we will notify you if legally permitted to do so).

6. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR)

You have the following rights over your data:

  • Access: View all your data at any time within the app.
  • Export: Download a complete copy of all your data in JSON format from Settings → Data & Privacy.
  • Correction: Edit any of your data directly inside Operix.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data. Email us at privacy@operix.app and we will process it within 30 days.
  • Withdrawal of consent: You may stop using Operix and request data deletion at any time.

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete all your business data within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics (with no personally identifiable information) may be retained for product analytics.

8. Cookies

Operix is a web application. We use only essential cookies required for authentication (Firebase Auth session token). We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party advertising cookies.

9. Children's privacy

Operix is a business platform intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users by email and/or an in-app notice at least 14 days before any material change takes effect. Continued use of Operix after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Grievance Officer (India — DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a Grievance Officer has been appointed to address privacy-related concerns from users in India.
